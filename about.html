About Sam Rabon

A dancer first. Then a studio owner. Now, private coaching.

I'm Sam Rabon. I've spent more than 40 years in movement. First as a professional dancer across Australia, Japan, Portugal, Italy, and France, then building, running, and selling studios in Sydney and Los Angeles, training and mentoring instructors along the way. Today I coach privately.

Nothing in the body moves alone. It's all connected, all reactive. Sessions are built around how you move in your daily life.

Movement has never really been the destination for me. What I've always been interested in is freedom. The freedom to participate fully in life, to stay engaged with the world, to keep saying yes to things that make me feel alive.
