Two modes, one system (2026-07-30 pt 3): Kasumi light is the default for every visitor; Kasumi Ink is one square away — the toggle at the right end of the booking band, remembered per visitor. Both grounds are flat (§34, F 2026-08-04): the haze→moss and ink→forest gradients are retired, and the footer is solid identity green. Image scrims on photography stay — they are legibility, not decoration. The header bar is identity green (F kept it, 2026-08-04); the retired bright green was the gradient grounds, not the nav. Every component styles itself with tokens, so nothing on this page (or the site) knows which mode it is in. Flip the square above to proof this page in both. Open item: Live Green sits on the dark ground by design — its behavior on the light ground is unreviewed; keep it inside dark surfaces (footer, dark modules) until it is.

  • Green is identity: footer, the patterns module, and testimonial names. It does not do hovers.
  • Persimmon is the signal layer: every label, every button outline, every hover fill, the current page in the nav. If it is orange, it orients or responds. Never body copy, never headlines.
  • One orange. White text on persimmon fills, always. Keep persistent persimmon text large enough to read on the gray; if a small line of it looks weak, the answer is layout, not a second orange.
  • One ground, two greens, one orange. Practice Green is identity, Live Green is the exception, Persimmon signals. Color is still not where this site gets its personality.
  • Live Green is the exception layer: reserved for the few moments that are genuinely live or scarce — live-session status, retreat access, first-access flags. Ink text on it, always. If it appears more than once per page, it has stopped being an exception.

Type

Two faces only. Archivo 900 for every display moment. Courier Prime for everything else: body, nav, labels, facts, captions. Four display steps, four mono steps, eight sizes on the whole site. There is no ninth. Case rule: titles and names are all-caps; spoken sentences (claims, quotes, testimonials) are sentence case at the same family, weight, and size — tracking -0.01em, leading 1.18. Caps stop at two lines; anything longer speaks.

Labels

Label · always persimmon Page eyebrow
  • All labels are persimmon, all the time, on every surface. There is no second label color.
  • Each inner page carries its glyph in the intro eyebrow: About/Hinge, Coaching/Step, On-Demand/Shift, Retreat/Reach, Library/Rotate.
  • No chips, no badges. A filled rectangle with padding is a button; nothing that only names things gets that shape. Module names live in caption labels.

Buttons

One component, one rule of context: on the gray, the CTA is outlined ink; on imagery and dark fields it is solid persimmon. Hover always swaps to the other state. The footer Subscribe is the same solid form. CTAs sit one type step above labels (--t-small, bold) so a control never dresses like a caption. The persimmon outline and text say pressable; the fill is the response. Inline references use .text-link. There are no other button or link styles. Placement: the CTA is the last thing in a module, card, or box, and it exits bottom-right (§33); the only exceptions are form submits and the 404 page. Copy: never "Work with Sam". The practice moves, it does not work. The canonical CTA is "Move with Sam".

Motion

  • One curve: --ease cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1). Three durations: --dur-fast 0.2s (color), --dur-med 0.35s (name color shifts), --dur-slow 0.6s (image scale, fade-ins).
  • Every rollover on the site resolves to persimmon. Current-page nav stays white: that is a state, not a rollover.
  • Image hovers scale 1.03–1.04, never more. The ticker runs 32s linear.
  • Everything honors prefers-reduced-motion.

Glyphs

STEP · HINGE · ROTATE · REACH · SHIFT. Verbatim from Sam's Movement Snacks for Daily Life on Solin, her actual method drawn, not an invented framework. One SVG sprite per page, referenced via <use>. Sizes: 14 (eyebrows, ticker), 40 (reserved), 88 (patterns module).

Pattern

One pattern, one job. The checker rule is the square dot repeated: a two-row checkerboard strip, 8px cells, for a structural seam where a hairline is not enough. Never behind text, never as a fill, never near the drawn glyphs. Live Green checker is reserved for genuinely live or scarce blocks, once per page at most. Status 2026-07-30: one placement — the 404 page, where a little fan-flag energy is earned. The footer-seam trial was pulled. Off-site (carousels, one-pagers, merch) it can go louder.

Marks

The bold counterpart to the drawn glyphs. Filled geometric forms, one language across fifteen marks, built for the sizes where a pencil line dies: favicon, app icons, avatars, section markers, Solin tiles, IG covers. Two tiers, one rule: drawn glyphs are editorial texture (patterns module, ticker, eyebrows), marks are interface and identity. Never both in the same module.

Space & shape

  • Two vertical rhythms only: --section clamp(5rem, 10vw, 9rem) inside padded text sections, --section-sm clamp(4rem, 8vw, 7rem) between full-bleed modules. --page-pad for horizontal padding, --gap 10px for bleed margins.
  • Zero border radius, site-wide. No pills, no rounded cards, square favicon, square dot.
  • Hairlines are rgba(28,28,26,0.12), always 1px, always full-width within their section.
  • Two breakpoints: 960px (layout stacks, mobile nav) and 560px (small-phone refinements).
  • Keyboard focus is a 2px persimmon outline, offset 2px, on every link, button, and input. Every page starts with a skip-to-content link, visible only on focus. In the nav, the current page holds the hover color: orange means you are here.
  • Every tap target is at least 24px tall: nav and footer links carry invisible padding, carousel dots have a 24px hit area around the 5px mark.
  • Three link forms and no more: the CTA, the label/nav link, and the underlined .text-link for inline references.

Editorial rules

  • No em-dashes anywhere. Middot (·) separates titles, labels, facts. Commas, colons, periods in prose. En-dashes stay in numeric ranges (30–40 min).
  • Never invent copy in Sam's voice. Live samrabon.com and her Solin pages are the source of truth. Flag the unverifiable.
  • No prices on this site. Solin owns pricing.
  • Verify at 1280px and 390px before every push.
