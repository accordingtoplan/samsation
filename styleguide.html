One system, six pages.

The living reference for samrabon.com. Everything on this page is rendered by the production style.css, so if it looks right here, it is right on the site. New work uses these tokens and components only. No raw hex values, no new font sizes, no new button styles.

  • Green is identity: footer, the patterns module, and testimonial names. It does not do hovers.
  • Persimmon is the signal layer: every label, every button outline, every hover fill, the current page in the nav. If it is orange, it orients or responds. Never body copy, never headlines.
  • One orange. White text on persimmon fills, always. Keep persistent persimmon text large enough to read on the grey; if a small line of it looks weak, the answer is layout, not a second orange.
  • One grey, one green, one orange. Color is not where this site gets its personality.

Type

Two faces only. Archivo 900, all-caps, for every display moment. Courier Prime for everything else: body, nav, labels, facts, captions. Four display steps, four mono steps, eight sizes on the whole site. There is no ninth.

Labels

Label · always persimmon Page eyebrow
  • All labels are persimmon, all the time, on every surface. There is no second label color.
  • Each inner page carries its glyph in the intro eyebrow: About/Hinge, Coaching/Step, On-Demand/Shift, Retreat/Reach, Library/Rotate.
  • No chips, no badges. A filled rectangle with padding is a button; nothing that only names things gets that shape. Module names live in caption labels.

Buttons

One component, one rule of context: on the grey, the CTA is outlined ink; on imagery and dark fields it is solid persimmon. Hover always swaps to the other state. The footer Subscribe is the same solid form. CTAs sit one type step above labels (--t-small, bold) so a control never dresses like a caption. The persimmon outline and text say pressable; the fill is the response. Inline references use .text-link. There are no other button or link styles.

Motion

  • One curve: --ease cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1). Three durations: --dur-fast 0.2s (color), --dur-med 0.35s (name color shifts), --dur-slow 0.6s (image scale, fade-ins).
  • Every rollover on the site resolves to persimmon. Current-page nav stays white: that is a state, not a rollover.
  • Image hovers scale 1.03–1.04, never more. The ticker runs 32s linear.
  • Everything honours prefers-reduced-motion.

Glyphs

STEP · HINGE · ROTATE · REACH · SHIFT. Verbatim from Sam's Movement Snacks for Daily Life on Solin, her actual method drawn, not an invented framework. One SVG sprite per page, referenced via <use>. Sizes: 14 (eyebrows, ticker), 40 (reserved), 88 (patterns module).

Space & shape

  • Two vertical rhythms only: --section clamp(5rem, 10vw, 9rem) inside padded text sections, --section-sm clamp(4rem, 8vw, 7rem) between full-bleed modules. --page-pad for horizontal padding, --gap 10px for bleed margins.
  • Zero border radius, site-wide. No pills, no rounded cards, square favicon, square dot.
  • Hairlines are rgba(28,28,26,0.12), always 1px, always full-width within their section.
  • Two breakpoints: 960px (layout stacks, mobile nav) and 560px (small-phone refinements).
  • Keyboard focus is a 2px persimmon outline, offset 2px, on every link, button, and input. Every page starts with a skip-to-content link, visible only on focus. In the nav, the current page holds the hover color: orange means you are here.
  • Every tap target is at least 24px tall: nav and footer links carry invisible padding, carousel dots have a 24px hit area around the 5px mark.
  • Three link forms and no more: the CTA, the label/nav link, and the underlined .text-link for inline references.

Editorial rules

  • No em-dashes anywhere. Middot (·) separates titles, labels, facts. Commas, colons, periods in prose. En-dashes stay in numeric ranges (30–40 min).
  • Never invent copy in Sam's voice. Live samrabon.com and her Solin pages are the source of truth. Flag the unverifiable.
  • No prices on this site. Solin owns pricing.
  • Verify at 1280px and 390px before every push.
